# them, and glibc, bionic and libSystem declare them alike. None of it is radare2's, so a
# refresh leaves it alone; see README.md. Every row carries `.include=` and sits under a
# comment naming its source, which `tests/cited_rows.rs` requires. A pointer's own qualifiers
# (`restrict`, `const` after the star) are not part of a prototype's type and are left out.
